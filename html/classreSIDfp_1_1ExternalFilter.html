Detailed Description

The audio output stage in a Commodore 64 consists of two STC networks, a low-pass RC filter with 3 dB frequency 16kHz followed by a DC-blocker which acts as a high-pass filter with a cutoff dependent on the attached audio equipment impedance. Here we suppose an impedance of 10kOhm resulting in a 3 dB attenuation at 1.6Hz. The STC networks are connected with a BJT based common collector used as a voltage follower (featuring a 2SC1815 NPN transistor).

To operate properly the 6581 audio output needs a pull-down resistor, 1KOhm is the recommended value.
